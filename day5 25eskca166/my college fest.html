About PRAVAHA

A celebration of innovation, technology, esports, sportsmanship, teamwork, creativity, and leadership.

PRAVAHA is the annual flagship college festival of Swami Keshwanand Institute of Technology. It brings together students, faculty, and guests through events that showcase competitive energy, collaborative spirit, and campus pride.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is PRAVAHA?

PRAVAHA is the annual flagship college festival of SKIT, bringing together innovation, technology, esports, sports, and culture.

Who can participate?

Students from SKIT and, where permitted, participants from other colleges can join the events and competitions.

How do I register?

Use the floating registration form on this page and submit the details for your selected event.

Are there certificates?

Yes. Participants and winners can receive certificates and prizes depending on the event category.

Can students from other colleges participate?

Yes, for events open to external participants. The rules will clarify eligibility for each category.

Will there be a schedule update?

The schedule section keeps the festival timeline visible and easy to follow on any device.
